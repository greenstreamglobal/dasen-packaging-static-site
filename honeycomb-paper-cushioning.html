Key Considerations for Protective Packaging Projects

Protective packaging decisions become clearer when product risk and packing method are defined upfront.

Fragility and Movement

Breakage risk, product movement inside the carton, and pressure points help determine whether cushioning or fitted inserts are required.

Product Weight

Light, medium-weight, and heavy products often require different paper structures and support levels.

Outer Pack Coordination

The internal protective structure should be discussed alongside the carton, dividers, and shipment layout, rather than in isolation.

Packing Efficiency

Effective protective packaging reduces damage risk without complicating assembly, packing, or repeat shipments.
