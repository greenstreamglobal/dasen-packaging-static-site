Key Considerations for Folding Carton Projects

Carton projects usually move faster when the retail role and print expectations are clarified before sample work starts.

Retail Role

Main shelf pack, inner carton, gift sleeve, or promotional box use can change the structure and material direction.

Artwork Load

Brand graphics, barcodes, ingredients, instructions, and compliance content affect layout and panel planning.

Finish Level

Simple retail print and higher-presentation finishing should be aligned early with the product position and budget.

Inner Fit

If the product needs inserts, dividers, or tray support, those points should be discussed together with the carton style.
