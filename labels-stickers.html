Key Considerations for Label Projects

These considerations are more valuable than reference galleries when defining the label solution.

Application Surface

Application surfaces (paper boxes, bottles, jars, bags, pouches, or plastic) influence material and adhesive selection.

Information Load

Branding, product data, barcodes, and regulatory content impact label size, layout, and print clarity requirements.

Finish Expectation

Matte, gloss, foil stamping, embossing, or utility labeling should align with product positioning and budget.

Application Method

Application methods (hand, line, roll, or sheet format) influence label structure from the outset.
