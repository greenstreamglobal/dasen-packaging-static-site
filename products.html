Applications and Industries

Packaging requirements vary by industry, and we provide customized solutions tailored to different product, market, and functional requirements.

Produce & Fresh Food

Corrugated cartons, stackable outers, and paper-based transport packaging for fruit, vegetables, and other fresh-packed products in short-cycle distribution.

Seafood & Frozen Food

Corrugated transport outers and protective packing structures for seafood, chilled goods, and frozen products requiring robust handling in cold-chain and moisture-controlled distribution.

Snacks & Processed Food

Printed retail cartons, shelf-ready packs, and branded packaging for snacks, dry food, bakery items, confectionery, and other processed food lines.

Retail & FMCG

Retail cartons, promotional packs, labels, and display-ready packaging for FMCG products requiring clean shelf presentation and stable repeat supply.

Cosmetics Packaging

Folding cartons, rigid boxes, paper bags, and fitted inserts for skincare, beauty, fragrance, and presentation-focused product sets requiring enhanced visual appeal.

Pharmaceutical Packaging

Cartons, manuals, folded inserts, and specification-compliant printed packaging for healthcare, supplement, and pharmaceutical programs requiring clear artwork and format control.

Electronics & Industrial

Protective cartons, corrugated outers, fitted inserts, manuals, and labels for electronics, tools, components, and industrial products requiring organized packing and transport protection.

Cross-Border E-Commerce

Mixed-format packaging solutions combining corrugated outers, retail cartons, bags, displays, and related printed materials for online retail, marketplace supply, and repeat replenishment programs.
